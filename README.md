# Gen AI-Powered Business Growth Assistant (Ongoing Project)  

An intelligent system designed to **unlock hidden business opportunities** by analyzing client-company interactions. This project addresses the common challenge that modern businesses often **miss growth opportunities due to ineffective client interactions**.  

---

## Project Overview
- **Goal:** Build an AI-powered assistant that extracts actionable insights from communication history and recommends relevant services from a company’s catalog.  
- **Approach:** Develop a pipeline that leverages:  
  - **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR):** Transcribes client-company meeting videos.  
  - **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Generate concise summaries, detect hidden business opportunities, and provide service recommendations.  

---

## Features
- Intelligent analysis of meeting transcripts to identify potential business opportunities.  
- Summarization of client interactions to reduce manual review time.  
- Suggest relevant products or services based on detected client needs.  
- Scalable architecture suitable for integrating with multiple company datasets.  

---

## Tech Stack
